# пинг таблет
Russian Telegram mini app for in-person table-tennis tournaments. Brand is always lowercase, two words: «пинг таблет».

- Main navigation: Главная / Сканировать / Рейтинг / Профиль. Tournaments remain accessible on Home and Profile.
- QR tournament registration, organizer-managed results; no standalone matchmaking or city selection.
- Ratings start at 100. Points and rating are distinct; no leagues.
- One player = one album card, regardless of acquisition. Exactly three album columns; unavailable cards grey. Preserve ownership on upgrades.
- Profile metrics: matches, wins, tournament wins. Role-based organizer/admin controls.
- Production uses Telegram authentication. Development fixture data must be marked as demo.
- Preserve safe-area handling, accessible forms, haptic/motion preferences, light and dark themes.
